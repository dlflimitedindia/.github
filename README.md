# DLF Privana North – Luxury Residential Development in Sector 76, Gurugram

[DLF Privana North](https://dlfltd.in/dlf-privana-north-sector-76-gurugram) is a premium residential project situated in Sector 76, Gurugram. Developed by DLF as part of its Privana township, the project focuses on spacious residential layouts, high-rise architecture and lifestyle facilities. It is an interesting development for homebuyers researching luxury apartments and penthouses in Gurugram.

## Project Overview

The residential real estate market in Gurugram has expanded significantly, with growing interest in planned communities and premium apartment developments. DLF Privana North is part of this evolving market and offers a high-rise residential environment in Sector 76.

The project covers approximately 17 acres and comprises six towers, each planned with 50 floors. It has a planned inventory of 1,164 residential units.
